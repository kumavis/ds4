## Scope and evidence

Branch `d22-session-memory` (handover head `20e0104`). Of the 18 short benchmark records, ten recorded checkout head `d12f025` and eight recorded `20e0104`. The intervening commit changed only the queue 2 pause check. Report 46's per-turn log analysis found that after the first ~20k tokens system memory mapped by the GPU was effectively flat through 132k plain and 98.6k DSpark. DSpark settled approximately 500 MiB lower than plain. Roughly 170 MiB of the difference appeared in GPU-mapped memory. Startup sizing varied by about 200 MiB and DSpark prefill sometimes consumed another 100–250 MiB temporarily.

The branch charges DSpark runtime at startup and can release expert cache slabs under pressure at a quiescent point. The rollback switch is `DS4_ROCM_STREAM_MEM_GUARD=0`. Historical model-free admission test output records PASS (0 failing) for injected host sizes, including admission at N and refusal at N+1. The remote queue log confirms the runtime 6,000-expert test refused before decode with exit 1. It needed 121.70 GiB against 121.42 available, a 0.28 GiB margin that depends on host state.
