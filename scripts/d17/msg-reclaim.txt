feat(d17b): partial staging reclaim for union sweeps (DS4_ROCM_STREAM_STAGING_RECLAIM_UNION, off by default)

With the union sweep, the staging stays lent and each layer takes back only
its routed expert positions in its parity half: entries living there are
evicted with D13's per-slot helper (cuda_stream_resident_evict_at), the
slots are held off the free list until the sweep ends, and a violation
counter (must stay 0) checks that no needed slot is still an entry's buffer
or allocatable. Refused (full D13 reclaim) when the D5 shelf is active.
Whole-layer sweeps keep D13's full reclaim; switch off behaves as before.
