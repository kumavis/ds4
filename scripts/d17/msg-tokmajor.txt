feat(d17b): token-major threshold as a setting (DS4_ROCM_V41_TOKEN_MAJOR_MAX, unset = stock 256)

On ROCm an append of fewer than N rows is prefilled token by token on the
warm decode cache instead of a layer sweep. Unset keeps the built-in 256;
N = 1 always sweeps. Token-major uses the decode kernels, so changing N can
change outputs (measured per turn against the stock twin).
